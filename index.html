How to play
  1. Find The Irish Guy's prediction video for the gameweek and post your scores in the comments.
  2. Put each match on its own line, for example Arsenal 2-1 Chelsea. Nicknames and common abbreviations work too.
  3. An exact score earns 3 points. The right result (win, draw or loss) earns 1 point.
  4. Post before kickoff. A prediction for a match that has already started doesn't count.
  5. Don't edit your comment after posting. Edited comments are disqualified for that gameweek.
  6. Small spelling slips in team names are reviewed by the admins, so they can still count.
